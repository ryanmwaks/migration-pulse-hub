Privacy, Terms & Safeguarding

How we collect, use, and protect your information — and our commitment to the safety and dignity of everyone who interacts with Migration Pulse Hub.

Last updated: May 2026

Privacy Policy Data Protection Terms of Use Safeguarding Cookie Policy
Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

Migration Pulse Hub is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who contacts us, uses our website, or participates in our programmes.

1. What Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, register for an event, or correspond with us, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • Your organisation or affiliation (if provided)
  • Your area of interest or type of support requested
  • The content of your message
  • Technical data such as browser type and IP address (for website security only)

We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as race, religion, health, or legal status) through our website forms. If you share such information in a message, it will be handled with the highest level of confidentiality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or request
  • Send you newsletters or updates you have subscribed to
  • Process event registrations
  • Manage volunteer or partnership applications
  • Improve our website and communications

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time and their data is removed within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Data Processors

We use the following third-party services that may process your data on our behalf. All processors are engaged under appropriate data processing agreements or equivalent safeguards:

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) — Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your IP address may be logged by GitHub as part of standard infrastructure operation. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • FormSubmit (99inbound Ltd) — Contact forms and event registration forms submitted on this site are processed by FormSubmit, which delivers submission data to our email inbox. See formsubmit.co/privacy.
  • Google LLC (USA) — Google Analytics & Fonts — We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website (anonymised usage data). We also load typefaces from Google Fonts servers (fonts.googleapis.com). Both services may process your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash / Fastly CDN — Some images on this website are served from images.unsplash.com, which is delivered via Fastly's content delivery network. This causes your IP address to be sent to Fastly/Unsplash infrastructure on page load. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website. Analytics data is anonymised and used solely for website improvement. We do not use Facebook Pixel, advertising networks, or behavioural profiling services.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data held by Migration Pulse Hub. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@migrationpulsehub.org. If you are based in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data Protection

Data Protection Statement

Migration Pulse Hub takes particular care with sensitive communications. Because our work involves migrants, refugees, and displaced persons who may be in vulnerable situations, we apply strict data protection standards to all communications we receive.

🔒
Sensitive communications: If you share information about your legal status, safety situation, or personal circumstances, this information will be treated as strictly confidential, shared only with the staff member directly assisting you, and never published or disclosed without your explicit written consent.

We use Google Analytics to understand site usage in an anonymised way but do not use tracking pixels or behavioural advertising. Third-party infrastructure services (GitHub Pages, Google Fonts, FormSubmit, and Unsplash image delivery) may process your IP address as a technical necessity — see Section 4 for details. We recommend using our official email address for sensitive communications rather than the contact form.

Safeguarding

Safeguarding Statement

Migration Pulse Hub is committed to creating and maintaining a safe environment for all people who interact with our organisation — including migrants, refugees, displaced persons, volunteers, staff, and partners.

🛡

Zero Tolerance for Harm

We have zero tolerance for any form of abuse, exploitation, harassment, or discrimination — whether involving staff, volunteers, partners, or the communities we serve.

Reporting Concerns

If you have a safeguarding concern related to our work or staff, report it to our designated Safeguarding Focal Point at safeguarding@migrationpulsehub.org. This is a confidential channel monitored separately from our general inbox. All reports are treated with strict confidentiality.

👶

Child Protection

All staff and volunteers working with children or young people are required to undergo safeguarding training and background checks before beginning work.

🔒

Confidential Communications

We treat all communications from people in vulnerable situations with the highest level of discretion and never share personal information without explicit informed consent.

Cookies

Cookie Policy

This website uses minimal cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or behavioural profiling tools.

Essential cookies used on this site include session cookies that expire when you close your browser. Third-party services we use — including Google Analytics, Google Fonts, FormSubmit, and Unsplash image delivery — may set their own cookies or use browser storage as part of their infrastructure. We do not control these cookies directly. You can review and disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.

We use Google Analytics 4 for anonymised site usage statistics. We do not use Facebook Pixel or behavioural advertising tools.

For a complete picture of third-party data transfers, please see Section 4 — Third-Party Data Processors above.

Questions About Privacy or Data?

Contact our team directly. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 working days.
