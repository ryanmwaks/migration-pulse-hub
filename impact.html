Areas of Impact

Where We Have Made a Difference

Our impact spans four interconnected areas — each reinforcing the others to create systemic change in migration governance.

🏛

Policy & Legislation

We have contributed to five pieces of national legislation and three regional policy frameworks across Kenya, Uganda, and Ethiopia. Our submissions to the Kenya Refugee Affairs Secretariat contributed to the 2021 regulatory amendments expanding work permit access for refugees.

  • Kenya Refugees Act (2021 Amendment) — contributed analysis
  • Uganda National IDP Policy — technical input provided
  • IGAD Regional Migration Policy — represented civil society
  • African Union Policy Framework Review — submitted position paper

Legal Protection

Our legal team has provided 2,300+ direct legal consultations and supported 18 strategic litigation cases — 12 of which resulted in favourable outcomes for migrants and displaced persons.

  • 480 individuals assisted with civil documentation
  • 230 asylum-seekers supported through application processes
  • 12 strategic cases with precedent-setting outcomes
  • 18 amicus briefs submitted to national and regional courts

Research & Knowledge

Our 18 published reports have been cited in parliamentary debates, UNHCR annual reports, and AU migration policy documents — translating field evidence into policy change.

  • 18 major research reports and policy briefs
  • 4 peer-reviewed academic journal articles
  • Cited in 3 UNHCR country reports
  • Knowledge Hub: 5,000+ resource downloads

Capacity & Community

Over 600 individuals have been trained across 48 workshops — building lasting local capacity that continues to protect migrant rights long after our direct involvement ends.

  • 350+ community paralegals trained in 5 countries
  • 48 training workshops and capacity sessions delivered
  • 12 civil society organisations strengthened
  • 240 government officials trained on refugee law
