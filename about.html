About Migration Pulse Hub

We are an advocacy, research, and legal action organisation committed to safeguarding the dignity and fundamental human rights of every person on the move — regardless of status, origin, or location.

Who We Are

A Rights-Based Organisation for the World's Most Vulnerable

Migration Pulse Hub is based in Nairobi, Kenya, and works at the intersection of migration, human rights, climate justice, health access, education, and technological innovation.

We tackle the underlying causes of forced displacement — not just its symptoms. We believe that every migrant, refugee, and internally displaced person is a rights-holder, not a charity case, and our work reflects that conviction in every programme we run.

We operate under the principle: "Mobility is not a crime. Rights move with people — everywhere."

Our Mission

To safeguard the dignity and fundamental human rights of internally displaced persons and international migrants by tackling the underlying causes of forced displacement — including climate justice, health access, education, and technological innovation.

We do this through rigorous research, strategic litigation, evidence-based advocacy, and capacity building that places communities at the centre of change.

🌅

Our Vision

A world where mobility creates opportunity and resilience rather than vulnerability — where all migrants and displaced individuals, regardless of status or location, possess protected rights and thrive within compassionate communities.

This vision is built on improved health outcomes, access to quality education, and lasting climate solutions that address displacement at its roots.

Core Values

The Principles Behind Everything We Do

Our values are not aspirational posters on a wall. They are operational commitments that shape every research paper, every court case, and every policy dialogue we engage in.

🌍

Inclusion

We centre migrants, refugees, and displaced people as rights-holders and decision-makers. We do not speak for communities — we amplify their voices.

🛡

Non-Discrimination

We oppose xenophobia, racism, and all forms of discrimination. Every person has universal human rights — full stop. Nationality, religion, or status does not change that.

🔍

Integrity

Transparency, accountability, and evidence-based work are non-negotiable. We publish what we find — not what is convenient — and we stand by our analysis.

⚖️

Justice

We believe in structural change, not band-aid solutions. Our advocacy targets the systems that criminalise mobility and exclude the displaced from rights and services.

🤝

Protection

Every action we take is guided by the conviction that protection is a legal obligation — not an act of charity — owed to every person regardless of movement.

💡

Dignity

Human dignity is the foundation of all rights. We refuse frameworks that dehumanise displaced persons and always lead with the humanity of those we serve.

"Rights Move with People Everywhere"

Wherever a person goes, their rights go with them. Our work exists to make that principle a lived reality — in courts, in parliaments, and in communities across the region.
