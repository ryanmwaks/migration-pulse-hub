East Africa Migration Rights Dialogue 2026

Training Kampala, Uganda Free Entry

Community Paralegal Training — Cohort 3 Launch (Kampala)

The third cohort of our flagship community paralegal training programme opens in Kampala. A six-week intensive covering refugee and migration law, documentation support, trauma-informed approaches, and referral pathways. Open to community members with a secondary school certificate.
