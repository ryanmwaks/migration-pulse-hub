World Refugee Day Dialogue 2026: Rights, Resilience and Return

📍 Nairobi, Kenya (Hybrid) 🕙 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM EAT 💰 Free 🗓 Registration: Open

An annual policy dialogue bringing together policymakers, civil society, researchers, and displaced community representatives to discuss the state of refugee protection in East Africa. The 2026 theme focuses on durable solutions and the right to voluntary, safe, and dignified return.

Community Paralegal Training — Refugee Rights & Legal Pathways

📍 Eastleigh, Nairobi 🕙 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM EAT 💰 Free (Lunch provided) 🗓 Spaces: Limited to 30

A full-day capacity training for community workers, faith leaders, and grassroots advocates. Covers international refugee law, the asylum process, legal referral pathways, and how to support displaced community members in navigating protection systems.

📍 Online (Zoom) 🕙 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM EAT 💰 Free 🗓 Registration: Open

An expert-led webinar examining existing and emerging legal frameworks for climate-displaced persons, with a focus on what protections are available, where the gaps are, and what advocates can do to push for stronger recognition at the domestic and international level.
