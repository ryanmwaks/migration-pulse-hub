Webinar Hybrid · Amnesty International Kenya Completed

75th Anniversary of the 1951 Refugee Convention: What Has It Delivered for Africa?

A commemorative Refugee Week Kenya webinar with refugee leaders, legal experts, academics, humanitarian practitioners, and advocates reflecting on the Convention's legacy, Africa's contribution to refugee law, remaining protection gaps, and the future of protection.

Migration Pulse Hub and OLAM Africa Research Institute launched their comparative study examining healthcare access for migrants facing climate pressure in Kenya and South Africa.
