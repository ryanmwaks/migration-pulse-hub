Organisational Background

Who We Are

Migration Pulse Hub (MPH) is an independent, Africa-rooted organisation dedicated to advancing the rights, dignity, and protection of refugees, migrants, and internally displaced persons. We are non-partisan, not-for-profit, and governed by an independent Board of Directors.

Through research-based policy engagement, strategic legal support, advocacy, and inclusive programming, MPH works to generate evidence, influence policy, and strengthen rights-respecting responses to mobility and displacement across the continent.

Our work centres on thematic priorities including climate justice, health, education, gender, and technology. We amplify the voices of affected communities and work to build community resilience and durable, people-centred solutions.
