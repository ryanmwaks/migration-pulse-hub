📋 Research Report 📅 March 2026 🌍 Kenya 📄 42 pages

Climate Mobility and Protection Gaps in Kenya: Migration Pulse Hub Report 2026

This report examines how climate-related displacement is affecting vulnerable communities in Kenya and highlights the legal, social, and policy gaps that require urgent attention. It draws on fieldwork in four counties and interviews with displaced households, local officials, and civil society actors.

Key Findings:
  • Climate displacement is increasing pressure on already vulnerable communities
  • Many affected persons lack clear legal protection pathways
  • Local-level coordination between agencies needs urgent strengthening
  • More disaggregated data is needed to support evidence-based policy decisions

📋 Field Study 📅 November 2025 🌍 Horn of Africa 📄 38 pages

Gender-Based Violence in Displacement: Evidence from the Horn of Africa

Field research documenting the prevalence and patterns of gender-based violence in displacement settings across the Horn of Africa, with policy and programme recommendations for governments, INGOs, and UN agencies.

Key Findings:
  • GBV rates in displacement settings are significantly underreported
  • Women and girls face compounding risks at every stage of displacement
  • Referral pathways for survivors are frequently broken or inaccessible
  • Community-based prevention mechanisms show the strongest evidence of effectiveness
