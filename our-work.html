Our Work

We protect migrants and refugees through four interconnected approaches: advocacy, research, litigation, and capacity building. Each reinforces the others — evidence informs advocacy, advocacy shapes policy, law enforces rights.

How We Protect

Protection Through Four Pillars

Human rights protection is a legal obligation — not charitable work. Our four protective approaches work in concert to create durable, systemic change for migrants and displaced communities.

🏛

Advocacy & Policy

We influence legislation and public discourse affecting migrants and refugees. Our policy team engages governments, parliaments, regional bodies, and international institutions to advance rights-based migration governance frameworks.

Our advocacy addresses the structural causes of forced displacement — not just its consequences.

Research

We produce evidence that combines quantitative data, field experience, and personal narratives to identify protection gaps and inform ethical decision-making in policy and programmes.

Our research is open-access and designed for use by policymakers, advocates, academics, and communities.

Advocacy

Eight Key Policy Focus Areas

Our advocacy is targeted and evidence-driven. We work across eight primary domains where legal gaps and policy failures create the greatest harm for migrants and displaced communities.

📜 1951 Refugee Convention Compliance

Ensuring states uphold non-refoulement principles and meet their obligations under the 1951 Refugee Convention and its 1967 Protocol.

🌐 Global Compact on Refugees

Supporting the implementation of the Global Compact on Refugees — translating its pledges into concrete national action plans and accountability mechanisms.

📋 Domestic Migration Legislation Reform

Reviewing and reforming domestic migration laws that criminalise irregular entry, restrict movement, or deny access to services for displaced persons.

🚫 Anti-Trafficking Enforcement

Strengthening legal frameworks and enforcement mechanisms to protect migrants from trafficking and labour exploitation — especially women and children.

♀️ Gender-Based Violence Prevention

Advocating for comprehensive legal and policy mechanisms to prevent GBV, support survivors, and hold perpetrators accountable in displacement contexts.

🏠 Asylum Procedure Improvements

Reforming asylum processes to be fair, accessible, and efficient — removing barriers that prevent refugees from accessing the protection they are legally entitled to.

🌡 Climate Displacement Legal Recognition

Advancing the legal recognition of climate-displaced persons and pushing for binding protection frameworks that address climate migration as the defining displacement crisis of our time.

📈 Data-Driven Protection Strategies

Advocating for evidence-based protection responses grounded in reliable disaggregated data on displacement causes, demographics, and outcomes.

Litigation

Strategic Legal Action That Changes Systems

A single court ruling can change the lives of thousands. Our litigation strategy focuses on cases with the greatest systemic impact.

We do not only defend individual cases — we select cases that challenge unlawful practices affecting entire classes of migrants or displaced persons, establishing precedents that bind future decisions.

Our Four Programmes

Integrated for Maximum Impact

Each programme operates as a standalone unit and as part of an integrated system. Evidence feeds advocacy, advocacy shapes law, law changes policy, policy transforms communities.

1. Rights-Based Migration Governance Accelerator

Influencing legislation and public discourse to advance rights-based migration governance frameworks that address displacement's root causes in conflict, climate, and inequality.

2. Impact & Transparency Legal Defense Initiative

Strategic legal action that defends individual rights, challenges unlawful practices, and establishes binding precedents that protect entire categories of vulnerable people.

3. Evidence-to-Action Research Hub

Rigorous research combining data, field experience, and personal narratives to identify protection gaps and generate the evidence base for all our advocacy and litigation work.

4. Rights & Resilience Policy Network

Building coalitions, shifting narratives, and mobilising collective action among civil society, governments, international bodies, and community-based organisations.
