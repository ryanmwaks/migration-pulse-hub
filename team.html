Our Team & Board

Migration Pulse Hub is led by a multidisciplinary team of lawyers, researchers, advocates, and community specialists — united by a shared commitment to human dignity and migrant rights.

Leadership

Executive Team

Our senior leadership team brings together expertise in international law, migration governance, research, and organisational management.

AM

Amina Mwangi

Executive Director

Barrister and migration law specialist with 18 years of experience in East African legal systems. Former UNHCR Senior Protection Officer. LLM (International Human Rights Law), University of Essex.

JO

James Ochieng

Director of Research

Migration researcher with a focus on climate displacement and statelessness. PhD (Migration Studies), Makerere University. Former research lead at the African Population and Health Research Center.

FK

Fatuma Kimani

Head of Legal Affairs

Advocate of the High Court of Kenya with 14 years specialising in refugee and human rights law. Former Legal Officer at the Kenya National Commission on Human Rights. LLB, University of Nairobi.

DA

David Abdi

Head of Advocacy & Policy

Policy specialist with 12 years of experience in AU, IGAD, and EAC processes. Former Policy Adviser to the IGAD Migration Desk. MSc (International Relations), University of Addis Ababa.

RN

Rose Nakato

Head of Programmes

Programme management specialist with 10 years in humanitarian and development contexts. Former Programme Manager at FHRI Uganda. MPA (Development Management), Uganda Management Institute.

SO

Samuel Otieno

Finance & Operations Manager

Certified Public Accountant (CPA-K) with 9 years in NGO financial management. Specialises in donor compliance and multi-currency financial reporting across East African jurisdictions.

Governance

Board of Directors

Our Board provides strategic oversight, fiduciary accountability, and independent governance — meeting quarterly and overseeing all major organisational decisions.

CN

Prof. Catherine Nduta

Board Chair

Professor of International Law, University of Nairobi. Former member of the African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child.

BM

Beatrice Mutua

Board Vice-Chair

Senior Civil Society Leader. Former Executive Director, East African Civil Society Forum. Expert in NGO governance and regional advocacy strategy.

IA

Ibrahim Ahmed

Board Treasurer

Chartered Accountant (ICAEW). Director of Finance, Pan African Development Foundation. Specialist in nonprofit financial governance and donor compliance.

LW

Dr. Lucy Wanjiku

Board Member

Migration researcher and former IOM consultant. PhD (Sociology), University of Cape Town. Author of three peer-reviewed books on East African migration.

Expertise

Advisory Council

Our Advisory Council comprises senior experts who provide technical guidance on specific thematic areas — meeting twice annually and available for consultation as needed.

PK

Climate & Displacement

HO

Halima Omar

Statelessness & Nationality

EG

Dr. Emmanuel Gitonga

Labour Migration
