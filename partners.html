Our Partners

We cannot protect migrants and refugees alone. Our partnerships with civil society, governments, international organisations, and academia multiply our reach and deepen our impact.

Partnership Philosophy

We Build Coalitions, Not Dependencies

Our partnerships are built on shared values — not just shared resources. Every organisation we work with is committed to rights-based approaches and the dignity of displaced people.

We seek partners who bring complementary expertise, reach, or resources. Whether you are a community-based organisation, an international donor, a university research centre, or a government agency — if you share our commitment to migrant and refugee rights, we want to work with you.
