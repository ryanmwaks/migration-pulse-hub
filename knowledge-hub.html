Legal Frameworks

Key International Instruments

The legal foundation of migrant and refugee protection — every person on the move has rights grounded in binding international law.

📜 1951 Refugee Convention

The cornerstone of international refugee protection, defining who is a refugee and the rights they are entitled to — including the principle of non-refoulement.

🌐 Global Compact on Refugees (2018)

A framework for more predictable and equitable sharing of responsibility for refugees — covering admission, protection, and durable solutions.

✈️ Global Compact for Migration (2018)

The first comprehensive intergovernmental agreement covering all dimensions of international migration in a holistic and comprehensive manner.

🌡 Kampala Convention (2009)

The African Union Convention on internal displacement — the first regional legal instrument specifically addressing the rights of internally displaced persons.
