Why We Exist

Because Protection Is Not Optional

When people are forced to move, systems must move to protect them. We fill the gaps where law, policy, and practice fail the world's most vulnerable.

⚖️

Legal Obligation

Protection is not charity — it is a right enshrined in international law. We enforce it through litigation, advocacy, and policy reform.

🔍

Evidence-Based Action

Rigorous, people-centered research reveals the protection gaps that drive our advocacy. Every campaign is grounded in data.

🤝

Community-Centered

We center migrants, refugees, and displaced persons as rights-holders and decision-makers — not passive recipients of aid.

Our Programs

How We Create Change

Four interconnected programs that move from evidence to action, from law to policy, and from community to coalition.

🏛 Rights-Based Migration Governance Accelerator

Influencing legislation and public discourse to advance rights-based migration governance frameworks that address displacement's root causes.

Learn More

⚖️ Impact & Transparency Legal Defense Initiative

Strategic legal action that defends individual rights, challenges unlawful practices, and establishes binding precedents for migrant protection.

Learn More

📊 Evidence-to-Action Research Hub

Producing rigorous research combining data, field experience, and personal narratives to identify gaps and inform ethical policy decisions.

View Reports

🌐 Rights & Resilience Policy Network

Building coalitions, shifting narratives, and mobilizing collective action among civil society, governments, and international institutions.

Impact Model

From Evidence to Systemic Change

Step 1

Ground Change in Evidence

Step 2

Strategic legal defense and impact litigation that translates rights into enforceable realities.

Step 3

Transform Policy

Evidence-based policy reforms that expand legal protection frameworks nationally and regionally.

Step 4

Mobilize Collective Action

Coalition-building and narrative shifting to create lasting political will for migrant rights.
