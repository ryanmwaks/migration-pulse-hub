Our mission: to ensure that the rights of every migrant and displaced person are recognised, respected, and upheld — regardless of borders.

Who We Are

Grounded in Rights, Driven by Evidence

Everything we do is anchored in international human rights law, community voices, and rigorous evidence.

🌍

Regional Focus

We work across East Africa — where displacement, labour migration, and climate-driven movement intersect at scale. Our programmes reach 8 countries including Kenya, Uganda, Ethiopia, and Somalia.

⚖️

Rights-Based Approach

We ground every intervention in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the 1951 Refugee Convention, the Kampala Convention, and the AU Migration Policy Framework.

🔬

Evidence-Led Action

Our research team generates primary data, publishes reports, and collaborates with academic institutions to produce credible, actionable evidence that shapes real policy change.

Our Work

Four Pillars of Action

Our work is organised around four mutually reinforcing pillars that address the full cycle of migration governance.

Pillar 1

Advocacy & Policy Engagement

We engage governments, regional bodies, and international institutions to shape migration policy. Our team participates in AU, IGAD, and EAC processes to embed migrant rights into frameworks that matter.

Learn More
Pillar 2

Research & Evidence Generation

We conduct field research, surveys, and case studies across East Africa to produce data that decision-makers need. Our Knowledge Hub makes this research freely accessible to all.

View Research
Pillar 3

Legal Aid & Litigation

Our legal team provides direct legal assistance — including case representation, documentation support, referrals, and strategic litigation where rights are systematically violated.

Legal Support
Pillar 4

Capacity Building

We train civil society organisations, community leaders, frontline workers, and local authorities on migration rights, trauma-informed care, and protection protocols.

Training Programmes
