# Contributing

This is a proprietary ZURI IT CONSULTS repository. It does not accept
unsolicited contributions.

Contributions are accepted only from people working under a written agreement
with ZURI IT CONSULTS that sets out who owns the work. Unless such an
agreement says otherwise, contributions made under it are assigned to
ZURI IT CONSULTS.

Pull requests from people without such an agreement will be closed without
review. To report a security issue, see SECURITY.md.
