Your Donation Makes Rights Real

We are an independent, non-profit organisation. Our work is funded entirely by donations, grants, and the generosity of people who believe migrants deserve dignity.

We publish audited financial statements annually. Every programme has a defined budget, a measurable outcome, and a reporting framework. You will know exactly what your money achieved.

Volunteer

Offer Your Skills

We welcome skilled volunteers across multiple disciplines. Volunteer roles are matched to specific programme needs — so your time makes a real difference.

Legal Volunteers

Lawyers, paralegals, and law students can assist with legal research, document review, client interviews, and case preparation under supervision of our legal team.

Research Volunteers

Researchers and data analysts can support literature reviews, field data analysis, report writing, and infographic production for our publications programme.

Communications

Writers, editors, designers, and social media managers can help us communicate our work more effectively — in English, Swahili, Somali, or Amharic.

💻

Technology

Web developers, data engineers, and digital security specialists can help us build and maintain secure digital infrastructure for our programmes and communities.

Training Facilitators

Experienced trainers and community facilitators can co-deliver our capacity building workshops — particularly those with expertise in adult learning and legal literacy.

Community Outreach

Community connectors who speak migrant community languages and understand local contexts can help us reach people who need our support most.
